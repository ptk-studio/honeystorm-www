Privacy

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

honeystorm.com is run by PTK Studio. It has no accounts and no ads. Each book has a page with a short feedback form (see Feedback).

Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

If you choose Allow in the banner, we count visits with Google Analytics 4 (via Google Firebase): which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors come from (country, referring site) and the device type. This helps us see what people find useful. If you choose No thanks, or your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, Google Analytics is never loaded and no analytics cookie is set.

Your choice is stored only in your browser. You can change it at any time: .

Google processes this data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Links to other sites

Links on this site (for example to stores, YouTube or Amazon) take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Amazon links

Some links to Amazon on this site are affiliate links: as an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases, and the pages that carry such links say so above them. Tapping one takes you to amazon.com, where Amazon's own privacy notice applies. We don't see who you are or what you buy; Amazon tells us only totals.

Feedback

If you send us feedback in HoneyStorm (through the feedback button, form or app screen), we receive your message, the kind of feedback you chose, an optional star rating, the page or app version you were using, and your language setting. On a book's page (honeystorm.com/books/…), the form asks for a rating from 1 to 5, what you liked and what we should change. We also receive the address of the page you sent it from, which tells us which book it's about. On a children's book's page, the form is for the grown-up: please don't include your child's name or anything about them; we don't ask for it. We ask for your email only if you want a reply, and we keep it only if you tick the box allowing us to contact you; we delete it after at most 90 days. We don't use cookies or tracking for feedback, and we don't collect device identifiers. We also store the IP address your feedback was sent from, to prevent abuse; it is deleted with your feedback after one year. Feedback is stored by PTK Studio's 88eggs service (hosted in the United States), used only to fix problems and improve HoneyStorm, never sold or shared, and deleted after one year. To have your feedback deleted sooner, contact us at hello@ptk-studio.com.
